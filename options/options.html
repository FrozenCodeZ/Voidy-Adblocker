          Stays on your computer

          • Blocking happens inside your browser, using rules stored on your computer.
          • Voidy has no account, no servers and no analytics. Your browsing is never sent anywhere.
          • Look-alike and dangerous-site checks run locally.
          • The only downloads are public block lists, and those carry nothing about you.

          Did you know?

          Start here

          New to ad blockers? Five minutes here and you'll know what every switch does.

          First, pin Voidy: click the puzzle-piece icon in your browser's toolbar, then the pin next to Voidy, so it's always one click away. See an ad that got through? Right-click it and choose Hide this with Voidy.
          It blocks

          Ads, trackers and known malware sites are stopped before they download. Pages load faster and fewer companies see you.

          It tidies

          Cookie banners (it clicks "Reject" for you), newsletter pop-ups, chat bubbles and "allow notifications?" nags disappear.

          It guards

          If a page tries to throw you to another site or open a pop-up by itself, you get asked first.

          Adapts when needed

          Auto starts in Full and steps up to Stealth when a site keeps showing an ad-block wall. Some sites can still tell.

          The one control you need: the mode

          Click the toolbar icon on any site. The four buttons change how hard Voidy works on that site only.

          OffNothing blocked. Use if a site you trust is broken.
          LiteBlocks ads & trackers at the network only. Gentlest.
          FullEverything in Lite, plus it hides empty ad boxes, pop-ups and surprise redirects.
          AutoStarts in Full and adds stealth when a wall persists. Recommended.

          If a site breaks

          1. Click the toolbar icon, then Site looks broken? Fix this site. Voidy asks a few Yes/No questions while it tests what it blocked there, then keeps allowing only what the site needs. To simply step down one level, use Or just ease off here.
          2. Still broken? Click it again, or pick Lite.
          3. Only the pop-ups are the problem (say, you need the site's chat)? Untick Hide pop-ups & widgets on this site.
          4. Last resort: Off for that site. Everything else stays protected.

          "Please disable your ad blocker"

          1. In Auto, just wait a second. Voidy usually notices and reloads once or twice in stealth.
          2. If the wall stays, the popup shows "This site keeps detecting the blocker" with choices.
          3. Max stealth also fools common detector scripts.
          4. Some walls can't be fooled. Then "Accept the wall" or reading the story somewhere else is the answer.

          The "Go there?" box

          It appears when a page tries to move you without you clicking a link. It shows where you'd go, the full address and any warning signs. Green means no red flags were found, amber means be careful, and red means it's likely a trick. Not sure? Press Stay here. Nothing happens unless you choose to go.

          Words you'll see

          Tracker
          A script that records what you do and reports it to a company, often across many sites.
          Filter list
          A public list of known ad/tracker addresses, maintained by volunteers (EasyList, EasyPrivacy…).
          Cosmetic filtering
          Hiding the empty boxes an ad would have filled, so the page doesn't have holes.
          Anti-adblock wall
          A page that hides its content until you turn your ad blocker off.
          Surrogate / fake
          A harmless stand-in file we give the page instead of the real ad script, so it thinks ads loaded.
          Stealth level
          How much disguising Auto is doing on a site, from 1 to 3. Blocking is the same at every level.
          Sensitive page
          Banks, payment providers and sign-in services, plus sites you add. Stealth tricks and redirect prompts stand aside there so nothing breaks.

            Stealth

            How Voidy avoids "disable your ad blocker" walls without letting ads through.

            In short: stealth blocks just as much as Full. For the few ad files detectors watch, it hands the page a harmless fake instead of a missing file, so the detector thinks ads loaded.

            The stealth ladder

            Full

            Normal blocking. Detectors see ad files missing.

            Stealth 1

            Watched ad scripts (Google Ads, Tag Manager, Analytics) are swapped for harmless fakes, whether the page loads them as a script or downloads them to check.

            Stealth 2

            Every test request to the big ad networks (images, downloads, beacons) gets an empty "200 OK" answer instead of an error.

            Stealth 3

            Also replaces well-known detector libraries (FuckAdBlock, BlockAdBlock…) with ones that always say "no ad blocker". Switches off by itself on pages with password or card fields.

            Auto may climb up to

            Auto only climbs when a blocking message persists. Loading a detector library alone does not change protection.
